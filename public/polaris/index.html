Why the angles match

Your horizon is perpendicular to straight up; the polar axis is perpendicular to the equator. Turn both sides of an angle by 90° and the angle doesn’t change.

Model assumes a spherical Earth and places Polaris exactly at the celestial pole (the real star is slightly offset).
