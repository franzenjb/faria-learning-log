Noon Walks West

Fix a moment: solar noon in New York. Noon is already finished everywhere to the east, and it has not yet arrived anywhere to the west. It walks west at 15° in an hour — about 13 miles a minute, or about 790 mph, at New York's latitude. Drag the slider, or press Play, and watch it go.
