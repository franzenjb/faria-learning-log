One Spot, Three Addresses

Click either map, or type a latitude and longitude. The same spot is written as latitude and longitude, as UTM, and, inside the United States, as State Plane.

The math runs in your browser with proj4js, the same formulas GIS software uses. Latitude and longitude are taken as WGS 84; for State Plane they are treated as NAD 83, which differs by a meter or two in the United States.
