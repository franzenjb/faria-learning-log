## Teaching Boundary
The photos/drawings do not share identical phase or orientation and are not generated by the interactive. No particular photographed feature is claimed to be the specific mountain Galileo measured. First-quarter means a quarter of the phase cycle, while about half of the visible disk is illuminated: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moon-phases/ .
