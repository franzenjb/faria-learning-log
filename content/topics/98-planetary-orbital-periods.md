A planet's year is one trip around the Sun. Earth's takes one year, by definition. The others run from under three months to well over a lifetime.

## 1. The periods

The **period** is the time for one full orbit. Distances are averages, in AU. One AU, one astronomical unit, is Earth's average distance from the Sun, about 93 million miles.

| Planet | Distance (AU) | Distance (miles) | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mercury | 0.387 | 36 million | 0.24 years (88 days) |
| Venus | 0.723 | 67 million | 0.62 years (225 days) |
| Earth | 1 | 93 million | 1 year |
| Mars | 1.524 | 142 million | 1.88 years |
| Jupiter | 5.20 | 484 million | 11.86 years |
| Saturn | 9.54 | 887 million | 29.46 years |
| Uranus | 19.2 | 1.78 billion | 84.01 years |
| Neptune | 30.1 | 2.80 billion | 164.8 years |

Neptune was found in 1846. It finished its first full orbit since then only in 2011.

## 2. One rule fits them all

Johannes Kepler found the pattern in 1619, from [Tycho Brahe's](../74-tycho-brahe-uraniborg-and-stjerneborg/) observations. In years and AU:

> *T*² = *a*³

*T* is the period. *a* is the average distance. Square means a number times itself (3² = 9). Cube means times itself twice (3³ = 27).

**Jupiter.** 5.2 × 5.2 × 5.2 ≈ 141. The square root of 141 is about **11.86**. So Jupiter's year is about 11.86 Earth years.

**Mars, as a check.** 1.52³ ≈ 3.51. √3.51 ≈ 1.87. The real period is 1.88 years. The gap is rounding.

## 3. Why the year grows faster than the distance

A farther planet has a longer path to travel, and it also moves more slowly. Both stretch the trip. So twice as far gives a year about 2.8 times as long, not twice as long. Jupiter is 5.2 times farther than Earth, and its year is almost 12 times longer.

## Key facts
- Mercury 88 days, Venus 225 days, Earth 1 year, Mars 1.88, Jupiter 11.86, Saturn 29.46, Uranus 84.01, Neptune 164.8 years.
- 1 AU is Earth's average distance from the Sun, about 93 million miles.
- Kepler's third law (1619): in years and AU, T² = a³.
- Jupiter: 5.2³ ≈ 141, √141 ≈ 11.86 years. Mars: 1.52³ ≈ 3.51, √3.51 ≈ 1.87, actual 1.88.
- Twice as far from the Sun means a year about 2.8 times as long.
