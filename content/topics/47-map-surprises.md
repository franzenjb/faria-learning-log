## 1. Due south from Havana misses South America
![Havana's meridian (82°W) runs south and misses South America; Recife lies at 35°W](../../img/Longitude_Havana_vs_Recife_Map_Tufte.png?v=1)
*Havana's meridian (82°W) runs south and misses South America; Recife lies at 35°W. Tufte-style map, Abbé Faria, 2026.*

Most mental maps put South America roughly below North America. It actually sits **far to the east**.
- **Havana: 82°W**
- **Lima: 77°W**, so even Lima, on South America's west coast, is **east of Havana**
- **Halifax, Nova Scotia: 64°W**
- **Recife, Brazil: 35°W**, so Recife is **east of Halifax**

Fly **due south from Havana** and you **clip western Panama**, then pass over **open Pacific just off Ecuador and Peru**. You never cross South America at all.

## 2. Europe sits far north
![European cities and the North American cities at the same latitude](../../img/Europe_America_Same_Latitude_Map_Tufte.png?v=1)
*European and North American cities at matching latitudes. Tufte-style map, Abbé Faria, 2026.*

Europe's great cities lie much farther north than their mild weather suggests. Match them by latitude:

| Europe | Latitude | North America | Latitude |
|---|---|---|---|
| London | 51.5°N | Quebec City (London is farther north) | 46.8°N |
| Paris | 48.9°N | St. John's, Newfoundland | 47.6°N |
| Rome | 41.9°N | Boston | 42.4°N |
| Madrid | 40.4°N | New York | 40.7°N |
| Algiers | 36.8°N | Raleigh | 35.8°N |

- **London is north of Quebec City.**
- **Paris** is level with **St. John's, Newfoundland**.
- **Rome** is level with **Boston**. Jeff guessed **Atlanta**, but Atlanta is at **33.7°N**, level with **Morocco** and **Beirut**.
- **Madrid** is level with **New York**.
- **Algiers**, in Africa, is level with **Raleigh, North Carolina**.

**Why Europe is so mild:** the **Gulf Stream** and its extension, the **North Atlantic Drift**, carry warm water from the tropics toward Europe, and the **prevailing westerlies** blow that ocean-warmed air onto the continent. Eastern North America, at the same latitudes, gets its winter winds from the frozen interior.

## Key facts
- Havana 82°W, Lima 77°W, Halifax 64°W, Recife 35°W: due south from Havana misses South America.
- London (51.5°N) is north of Quebec City (46.8°N); Rome ≈ Boston; Madrid ≈ New York.
- The Gulf Stream, North Atlantic Drift and westerlies keep western Europe mild.
