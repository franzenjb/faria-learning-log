A short word lesson: [QED](/topics/99-qed-the-end-of-a-proof/) is Latin *quod erat demonstrandum*, "which was to be shown," written at the end of a proof. Euclid used the Greek *ὅπερ ἔδει δεῖξαι*; many books now use a square, ∎, instead.

A longer lesson from the same day: [Celestial Navigation and Earth's Geometry](/topics/100-celestial-navigation-and-earth-geometry/), on Polaris's changing distance from the true pole, the Regiment of the North Star, Columbus, why a degree of longitude shrinks as you go north, the Southern Cross as a southern pointer, and how the trade winds and westerlies did half the steering.
