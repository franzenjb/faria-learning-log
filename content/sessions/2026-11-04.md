## Timeline
The *Lives* run about 1,200 years with legend (Theseus c. 1200 BC to Galba and Otho, AD 69), with a historical core of about 600 years (Solon to Antony and Cleopatra). Plutarch wrote c. AD 100–120. Like writing lives from the Vikings to Reagan.

## Cincinnatus
Dictator in 458 BC, victorious in about 15–16 days, resigned with about 5.5 months unused; again around age 80 in 439 BC. The model for Washington, "the Cincinnatus of the West," the Society of the Cincinnati and Cincinnati, Ohio. His name means "curly-haired."
