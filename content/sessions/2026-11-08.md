---
title: "Nov 8: hurricane wind force, a square and not an explosion"
---
Jeff's 8 November question started from a hurricane wind field and from the habit of calling the force exponential. On a comparable wall, in comparable air, the push follows the square of the speed. Equal steps in miles per hour add larger pieces of force, and they do not multiply that force by one fixed factor. The fixed factor is the exponential pattern, the one in compound interest. The power flowing in the wind is a third measurement, and it follows the cube.
