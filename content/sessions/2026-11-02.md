title: "Nov 2: Typhoon scales, Paradise Lost, Delphi and Plutarch"
---
A morning question about how typhoons are ranked, then on to the first of the creature's three books: Milton's *Paradise Lost*, from the blind poet and Satan's speeches to the cosmos, the churches' verdicts and the quiet, beautiful ending. Then the second book, Plutarch's *Lives*, which opened a long detour into Delphi, its oracle and the religion behind it, before we began the first pair, Alexander and Caesar.
