title: "Nov 11: Jupiter's Moons, Ground Speed, and Planetary Years"
---
Callisto's fixed distance from Jupiter, seen from the [near and far sides of Earth's orbit](/topics/96-jupiters-moons-as-a-measuring-stick/), looks about 10 and about 7 arcminutes wide: a scale in AU, not miles, and the same stretch Rømer later timed with Io's eclipses. Then two number lessons: [eastward ground speed](/topics/97-eastward-ground-speed-and-the-cosine-of-latitude/) falls as the cosine of latitude, 1,040 mph at the equator to 520 at 60°, and the [planets' orbital periods](/topics/98-planetary-orbital-periods/) run from 88 days to 164.8 years, all on Kepler's T² = a³.
