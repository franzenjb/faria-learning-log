A set of lessons on the word-type terms that are easy to mix up: acronym, homonym, and the rest. The hub is [Confusing Word Terms: Acronym, Homonym, and the Rest](/topics/133-confusing-word-terms/). Four grouped pages follow: [Shortened Words](/topics/134-shortened-words-abbreviation-to-backronym/) (abbreviation, contraction, acronym, initialism, backronym, and the myths that *posh*, *SOS*, and *golf* are acronyms); [Same Sound, Same Spelling, or Neither](/topics/135-homonym-homophone-homograph-heteronym/) (homonym, homophone, homograph, heteronym, and capitonym, with a chart of sound by spelling); [The -nym Family](/topics/136-nym-family-synonym-to-retronym/) (synonym, antonym, contronym, eponym, toponym, demonym, and retronym); and [Wordplay and Slips](/topics/137-wordplay-and-slips/) (portmanteau, onomatopoeia, palindrome, anagram, malapropism, eggcorn, mondegreen, spoonerism, oxymoron, euphemism, and neologism).
