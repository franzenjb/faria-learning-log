A lesson on Franz Kafka's 1915 story: [The Metamorphosis: Kafka's Salesman Who Woke Up Useless](/topics/122-the-metamorphosis/). Gregor Samsa wakes as an *ungeheueres Ungeziefer*, a word English cannot quite translate, and Kafka asked his publisher that the insect never be drawn. Over three parts, three escapes from his room (the chief clerk, the apple, the violin), his family stops needing him and goes back to work, and after his death they ride the tram into the country. The story picks up Dostoevsky's insect wish from [Notes from Underground](/topics/121-notes-from-underground/), and it ends with a reading for disaster work: what a catastrophe reveals about what a household was already carrying. The web page is a short summary; the full lesson is the [PDF](/downloads/The_Metamorphosis_Kafka_1915_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-23.pdf).
