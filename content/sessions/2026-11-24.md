title: "Nov 24: Ancient Parallels, Socrates, Why?, Aristotle, the Doctrine of the Mean, and Dionysius Exiguus"
---
Six lessons from the same day. First, [Ancient Parallels: How Mesopotamian Cultures Shaped the Old Testament](/topics/123-ancient-parallels-mesopotamia-old-testament/): four story parallels (the flood, creation, law, and wisdom), a Babylonian field problem, and a coda on Omar Khayyam and FitzGerald's *Rubáiyát*. Then [Socrates: The Gadfly of Athens](/topics/124-socrates-gadfly-of-athens/): his life and method, the trial of 399 BCE, the refusal to escape, the last words, and a short look at the cycle of governments in Plato's *Republic*. Then [Why?: Questioning Authority from Socrates to Darwin](/topics/125-why-questioning-authority-socrates-to-darwin/): one question followed from Socrates through Aquinas, Erasmus, Luther, and Galileo to Darwin. Then [Aristotle: Master of Those Who Know](/topics/126-aristotle-master-of-those-who-know/): his life and method, the ethics of the mean, the four causes, the break from Plato, and a section on where he was wrong and why those errors lasted. Then [Aristotle's Doctrine of the Mean: Virtue Between Two Extremes](/topics/127-aristotle-doctrine-of-the-mean/): seven virtues, each between too little and too much, treated as everyday tools. Then [How a Monk Set Year One: Dionysius Exiguus](/topics/128-dionysius-exiguus-how-a-monk-set-year-one/): a sixth-century monk labeling Easter tables, a year one a few years late, what Bede did and did not see, and how the number spread into law. The web pages are short summaries; the full lessons are the PDFs, [Ancient Parallels](/downloads/Ancient_Parallels_Mesopotamia_Old_Testament_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-24.pdf), [Socrates](/downloads/Socrates_Gadfly_of_Athens_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-24.pdf), [Why?](/downloads/Why_Questioning_Authority_Socrates_to_Darwin_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-24.pdf), [Aristotle](/downloads/Aristotle_Master_of_Those_Who_Know_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-24.pdf), [the Doctrine of the Mean](/downloads/Aristotle_Doctrine_of_the_Mean_Virtue_Between_Extremes_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-24.pdf), and [Dionysius Exiguus](/downloads/Dionysius_Exiguus_How_a_Monk_Set_Year_One_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-24.pdf).
