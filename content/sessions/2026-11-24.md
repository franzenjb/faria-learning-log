A lesson on how older Mesopotamian stories stand behind the Hebrew Bible, and how the Hebrew writers recast them rather than copy them: [Ancient Parallels: How Mesopotamian Cultures Shaped the Old Testament](/topics/123-ancient-parallels-mesopotamia-old-testament/). Four parallels: the flood in Gilgamesh Tablet XI and Genesis 6–9 (the boat, the birds, the sacrifice); *Enuma Elish* and Genesis 1 (a body split, or a word spoken); Hammurabi's code and the Covenant Code in Exodus 21–23 (the goring ox, eye for eye, a king under Shamash against a covenant from God); and Job and Proverbs beside Babylonian wisdom, with a nod to Egypt's *Instruction of Amenemope*. A coda on Omar Khayyam of Nishapur: the geometric cubics, the Jalali calendar, and two quatrains from FitzGerald's *Rubáiyát*. The web page is a short summary; the full lesson is the [PDF](/downloads/Ancient_Parallels_Mesopotamia_Old_Testament_Faria_Lesson_2026-11-24.pdf).
